## Title
A tree trunk cried like a child… and the whole mosque heard it 🥺

## Description
In the Prophet's ﷺ mosque in Madinah, there was an old palm trunk. He ﷺ would stand beside it to give the sermon. Then a pulpit was built for him, and he stood on it instead.

And the trunk began to cry, like a child, so loudly that everyone in the mosque heard it. The Prophet ﷺ came down from the pulpit and held it until it became calm. He said it cried for the remembrance of Allah that it used to hear.

Sahih al-Bukhari 3584
