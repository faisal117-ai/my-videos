The hadith of the three men trapped in a cave (Sahih al-Bukhari 2272): a rock sealed them in, and each asked Allah by one deed he did only for Him.

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ told us of three men who took shelter in a cave. A rock rolled down and closed it. They said: only our good deeds, done for Allah alone, can save us now.
• The first served his old parents' milk before anyone else, and waited all night until dawn.
• The second left a sin, only out of fear of Allah.
• The third grew a worker's forgotten wage into a whole herd, and gave him all of it.
Each time, the rock moved. Then it rolled away.

A good deed no one sees… Allah sees. Do one today, only for Him.
Source: Sahih al-Bukhari 2272 (also Sahih Muslim 2743).
