## Title
An entire army was marching… and one tiny ant saw it coming 🐜

## Description
Hazrat Sulaiman (AS) was marching with his army of men, jinn and birds. When they reached the valley of the ants, one ant cried out:

"O ants, enter your dwellings that you not be crushed by Solomon [Sulaiman] and his soldiers while they perceive not."
Surah An-Naml 27:18 (Sahih International)

Sulaiman (AS) heard her and smiled, and he prayed:
"My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favor…" (27:19)

The most powerful king answered the smallest voice with gratitude.
