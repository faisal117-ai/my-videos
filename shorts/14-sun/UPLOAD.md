## Title
The man Allah stopped the sun for… ☀️

## Description
One of the prophets marched his army to a city late in the day, as the sun was about to set. If it set, the battle would have to stop. So he said to the sun: "You are commanded, and I am commanded. O Allah, hold it back for us." And the sun was held back until Allah gave them victory.

Sahih al-Bukhari 3124 (the prophet is not named in this narration, so we do not name him either).
