Prophet Sulaiman (AS) made a dua: "My Lord, forgive me and grant me a kingdom such as will not belong to anyone after me. Indeed, You are the Bestower." (Saad 38:35) Forgiveness first, then the kingdom.

Allah gave him the wind and a spring of copper (38:36, 34:12), with one command: "Work, O family of David, in gratitude. And few of My servants are grateful." (34:13)
Before you ask Allah for more today, ask Him for forgiveness first. Astaghfirullah.
