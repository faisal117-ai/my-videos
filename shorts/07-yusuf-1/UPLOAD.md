## Title
His own brothers threw him into a well… | Yusuf (AS) Part 1

## Description
As a child, Hazrat Yusuf (AS) saw a dream: eleven stars, the sun and the moon, prostrating to him (Yusuf 12:4).
His father, Hazrat Yaqub (AS), told him: do not tell your brothers (12:5).
But his brothers were jealous. They took him and threw him into the bottom of a well (12:8–15).

Then Allah inspired him:
"You will surely inform them [someday] about this affair of theirs while they do not perceive."
Surah Yusuf 12:15 (Sahih International)
