## Description
Hazrat Yunus (AS) left his people in anger. A storm came, he was thrown into the sea, and the fish swallowed him (37:142). Darkness of the night, of the sea, and inside the fish.

"There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers." (21:87)
