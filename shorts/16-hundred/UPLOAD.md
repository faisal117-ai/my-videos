## Description
A man had killed ninety-nine people. He asked a monk if he could be forgiven; the monk said no, and he killed him too. Then a scholar told him: "Who can stand between you and repentance?" and sent him to a land where people worshipped Allah. He died on the way. The land was measured: he was closer to the good land by a hand's span, and Allah forgave him.

Sahih al-Bukhari 3470 and Sahih Muslim 2766 (the scholar's words are from Muslim's narration).
