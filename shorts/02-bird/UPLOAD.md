## Description
On a journey, the Companions found a small bird with two chicks, and they took the chicks. The mother came back, flapping her wings and circling over them. When the Prophet ﷺ saw her, he said:

"Who has distressed this bird by taking her young? Return her young to her."
Sunan Abi Dawud 2675
