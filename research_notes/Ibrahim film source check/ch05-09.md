## ch05: The fire

### Takeaway
The Quran gives the command to burn him (21:68, 37:97), the fire's order (21:69) and the outcome (21:70, 37:98). It does not mention days of gathering wood, a fire no one could approach, a catapult, or "burned his ropes". Those details come from tafsir and Israiliyat. A sahih replacement for the ropes line is Ibrahim's words "Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel", which he said when he was thrown into the fire (Bukhari 4563).

## ch06: The king who argued (2:258)

### Takeaway
2:258 gives the debate itself. It does not say the debate came after the fire, or that news reached the king. "I can pardon a man or have him killed" is the tafsir explanation (the two-prisoners story, from Mujahid and others). The Quran quotes only "I give life and cause death." The sun wording is exact.

## ch08: Hajar and Ismail in Makkah (Bukhari 3364)

### Takeaway
Most of ch08 matches Bukhari 3364: a leather bag of dates and a small water-skin; "Has Allah ordered you to do so?" "Yes." "Then He will not neglect us"; the 14:37 dua; seven runs between Safa and Marwa; and the Prophet ﷺ saying this is the origin of people's sa'y between them. Two lines are wrong. Zamzam came out when an angel dug the ground with his heel (or his wing), not "at the baby's feet". And Ibrahim faced the Ka'bah and raised his hands. The hadith does not say he "turned to the sky".

## ch09: The four birds (2:260)

### Takeaway
2:260 is quoted exactly. "Cut them into pieces" is NOT in the Sahih International text. It reads "commit them to yourself. Then [after slaughtering them] put on each hill a portion of them". The slaughter is only an interpretive bracket. The Quran states "they will come [flying] to you in haste" as Allah's promise. It does not narrate the event. Line 09.1 has a factual error: Ibrahim was not present when Zamzam appeared (Bukhari 3364), so "he had seen water rise from a desert" is wrong.

### Cited Findings
| Line | Source | Verdict | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 09.1 "had seen the fire turn cool. He had seen water rise from a desert." | Fire: 21:69. Water: in Bukhari 3364 Ibrahim had already left, and he learns of Zamzam on a later visit. | NOT (2nd sentence) | "Ibrahim, alayhis salaam, had seen the fire turn cool. He had seen his Lord answer his duas. And still, he asked for one more thing." |
| 09.2 "My Lord, show me how You give life to the dead." | 2:260, exact | OK | n/a |
| 09.3 "have you not believed?... Yes, but only that my heart may be satisfied." | 2:260, exact (minus the bracket "[I ask]") | OK | n/a |
| 09.4 "take four birds, cut them into pieces, put a part of them on each hill. Then call them." | 2:260: "Take four birds and commit them to yourself. Then [after slaughtering them] put on each hill a portion of them; then call them". "Cut them into pieces" goes beyond the text and turns the bracket into narration. | ~ / NOT as worded | "So Allah told him: take four birds, and draw them close to you. Then put a portion of them on each hill. Then call them." |

Use it only after checking the wording. |

- 2:260 wording: local Sahih International file; [Quran 2:260](https://quran.com/2/260)
- [Bukhari 3372](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3372) "We are more entitled to doubt than Abraham" (not re-fetched; verify)

### Inferences
- 09.4 and 09.5 can be merged so that the Quran's own promise carries the climax, without narrating an outcome the verse does not state.

### Gaps
- No sahih hadith describes the birds actually returning. Reports that they returned come from tafsir.
