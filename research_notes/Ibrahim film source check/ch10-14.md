# Ibrahim film source check: chapters 10–14

Method: every line of `long/03-ibrahim/narration/ch10.json` … `ch14.json` checked against the Sahih International text in `node_modules/quran-json/dist/quran_en.json` (verbatim), and against Bukhari/Muslim. Note: sunnah.com and the other hadith APIs are blocked in this session's network, so hadith wording was confirmed from search-result snippets of sunnah.com / tohed.com pages, not a direct page read (see Gaps).
Verdicts: **OK** = stated in Quran/sahih · **FIX** = not in source (embellishment, tafsir or custom); a replacement is given · **minor** = fine in substance, wording tweak suggested.

## Ch10: The dream and the sacrifice (37:99-113)

### Takeaway
Lines 1, 3–7 match 37:102-107. Line 2's "the dreams of prophets are revelation" is the statement of the Tabi'i 'Ubaid ibn 'Umair recorded in Bukhari 138, not a hadith of the Prophet ﷺ. Replace it with wording that comes from the verse itself. Line 8 is fine as a present-day observation; soften "loved Allah more than anything".

## Ch14: The lesson (60:4, 37:109, Bukhari 3370, Bukhari 4563)

### Takeaway
The salawat (Bukhari 3370), 37:109 and Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel (Bukhari 4563: Ibrahim said it when thrown into the fire) are all confirmed. Three lines need changes. Line 2's "every time, he was alone" contradicts the sources, since Isma'il built with him and Hajar was in the valley. Line 1's "a House he would never see filled" is speculation. The 60:4 quote should match SI ("excellent pattern… and those with him").
