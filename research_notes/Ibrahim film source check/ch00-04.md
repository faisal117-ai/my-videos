# Ibrahim (AS) film: source check of ch00–ch04 narration

Quran quotes are taken word for word from the local Sahih International text (`node_modules/quran-json/dist/quran_en.json`), with links to [quran.com](https://quran.com). Hadith references are from Sahih al-Bukhari, numbered as on sunnah.com. sunnah.com and alim.org could not be fetched from this session (DNS blocked), so the hadith wording comes from search-result excerpts and should be checked once on sunnah.com before publishing.

Verdicts: **OK** = stated in Quran or sahih hadith. **OK~** = in source, but the wording or a small detail differs (minor fix suggested). **NOT** = not in Quran or sahih hadith (tafsir, Israiliyat or embellishment). Each NOT line gets a replacement.

## ch00.0: the birds falling from the sky, and the full table

### Takeaway
The birds line is NOT in the Quran or sahih hadith. It comes from the tafsir and qisas literature (Ibn Kathir's retelling), and Ibn Kathir himself relays it from earlier reports. The best replacement hooks are 37:97 (the furnace) or Bukhari 4563 ("Hasbunallah"). Of the other 37 lines, 5 more are NOT in source and about 9 need small wording fixes. The rest are fine.

### Cited Findings
- Quran 37:97: "They said, 'Construct for him a furnace and throw him into the burning fire.'" Quran 21:68: "Burn him and support your gods - if you are to act." Quran 29:24: "Kill him or burn him," but Allah saved him from the fire. None of these mentions birds. — [Quran 37:97](https://quran.com/37/97), [Quran 21:68](https://quran.com/21/68), [Quran 29:24](https://quran.com/29/24)
- A text drawn from Ibn Kathir's history of the prophets says the fire was so intense that birds passing by it would burn. It is a qisas/tafsir report, not a hadith. The page does not clearly name its book, so the attribution is likely but not confirmed. — [hadithunlocked (Ibn Kathir history excerpt)](https://hadithunlocked.com/history:118)
- In Ibn Kathir on 21:69, Qatadah says every creature tried to put out the fire except the gecko. This is a Tabi'i's report, not marfu'. — [Ibn Kathir 21:69 on quran.com](https://quran.com/al-anbya/69/tafsirs/en-tafisr-ibn-kathir)
- Bukhari 4563: Ibn Abbas says "Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakil" was said by Ibrahim when he was thrown into the fire. Bukhari 4564 says these were his last words as he was thrown in. — [Bukhari 4563](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4563)
- Bukhari 3359: the Prophet ﷺ ordered geckos killed and said one blew on Ibrahim's fire. An Islamweb fatwa notes that part of this wording may be a narrator's (Ibn Jurayj's) explanation. **Do not use it as a hook.** — [Bukhari 3359](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3359), [Islamweb fatwa 363838](https://www.islamweb.net/en/fatwa/363838/command-to-kill-geckos)

### Inferences
- Lines NOT in source (need replacing): ch00.0, ch01.0, ch01.1, ch01.5, ch02.0 (first sentence) and ch04.3.
- Minor wording fixes: ch00.1, ch00.4, ch00.5, ch01.3, ch01.6, ch02.2, ch02.3, ch03.0, ch03.4 and ch04.8.
- Option B for ch00.0 (Bukhari 4563) best fits the channel's core message (trust in Allah / dua). Option A (37:97) is closest to the old line's visual spectacle. If Option B is used, ch00.1 should not repeat the words "my Lord is Allah".
- The ch04 order follows Surah al-Anbiya. Swapping in the 37:88-90 "I am ill" detail for the festival line keeps the plot (the town leaves, he goes in alone) using only Quranic material.

### Gaps
- I could not read the exact sunnah.com wording of Bukhari 3350, 3357, 3359 and 4563 (sunnah.com and alim.org were blocked here). The numbering and content come from search excerpts that agree with each other. Check the wording before quoting a hadith on screen.
- I did not locate the original isnad of the birds report or confirm which early narrator (e.g. as-Suddi) it comes from. It is clearly absent from the Quran and the Sahihayn.
