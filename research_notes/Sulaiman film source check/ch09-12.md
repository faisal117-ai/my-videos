## Q1: Throne, ifrit, the one with knowledge, glass palace, the queen's worship (27:24, 27:38–44)

### Takeaway
All of ch09–10 is grounded in 27:38–44. There are three content slips: "a man… from the Book" (SI: "one who had knowledge from the Scripture"), "deep water" (SI: "a body of water") and "palace of glass over water" (tafsir only). The sun-worship line is supported by 27:24 and 27:43.

### Cited Findings
- SI 27:39 reads "A powerful one from among the jinn said, 'I will bring it to you before you rise from your place, and indeed, I am for this [task] strong and trustworthy.'" SI does not use "ifreet" — [corpus.quran.com 27:39](https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=27&verse=39), [legacy.quran.com 27:39](https://legacy.quran.com/27/39)
- SI 27:40: "Said one who had knowledge from the Scripture, 'I will bring it to you before your glance returns to you.'" Mohsin Khan renders it "within the twinkling of an eye" — [legacy.quran.com 27:40](https://legacy.quran.com/27/40), [corpus.quran.com 27:40](https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=27&verse=40)
- SI 27:42: "[It is] as though it was it." Pickthall: "as though it were the very one" — [corpus.quran.com 27:42](https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=27&verse=42)
- SI 27:43: what she worshipped other than Allah "had averted her" — [corpus.quran.com 27:43](https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=27&verse=43)
- SI 27:44: "She was told, 'Enter the palace.'... she thought it was a body of water... 'a palace [whose floor is] made smooth with glass'... 'I submit with Solomon to Allah, Lord of the worlds'" — [corpus.quran.com 27:44](https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=27&verse=44), [legacy.quran.com 27:38-44](https://legacy.quran.com/27/38-44); tafsir on *ṣarḥ* (palace of or inlaid with glass): [tohed.com 27:44](https://en.tohed.com/tafsir/27/44/)
- 27:24 ("prostrating to the sun instead of Allah") and 27:38 — standard SI text, not re-fetched: [quran.com 27:24](https://quran.com/27/24), [quran.com 27:38](https://quran.com/27/38)

### Inferences
- "Before your eye can blink" is an acceptable narrator gloss because several translators render it that way. The quoted card, however, must stay "before your glance returns to you".
- "Over water" (10.3) comes from the tafsir picture of glass laid over flowing water. The verse supports only "she thought it was water".

### Gaps
- Could not open quran.com itself to copy 27:38 and 27:24 character for character (host blocked).

## Q2: Death and the staff, the creature of the earth, the jinn and the unseen (34:14)

### Takeaway
Lines 11.1, 11.3 and 11.4 quote 34:14, but 11.1 has "showed the jinn" where SI has "indicated to them [i.e., the jinn]". Line 11.2 ("still upright on his staff, the jinn kept working") is a fair implication of the verse; "upright" and any duration are tafsir.

### Cited Findings
- SI 34:14: "And when We decreed for him [i.e., Solomon] death, nothing indicated to them [i.e., the jinn] his death except a creature of the earth eating his staff. But when he fell, it became clear to the jinn that if they had known the unseen, they would not have remained in humiliating punishment." — [quran.com 34:14 SI](https://quran.com/saba/14/translation/en-sahih-international), [legacy.quran.com 34:14](https://legacy.quran.com/34/14)
- In tafsir the creature is a termite or wood-worm, the staff became hollow, and his body fell; the duration comes from tafsir as well — [Ibn Kathir 34:14](https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-14-sora-34.html)

### Inferences
- "When he fell" after the staff was eaten implies that he was supported by the staff. "Remained in humiliating punishment" implies that the jinn kept working, so 11.2 is acceptable. "Leaning on his staff" is closer to the text than "upright".

### Gaps
- No sahih hadith on the details of Sulaiman's death was found or needed.

## Q3: Qarun (28:78, 28:81), Ibrahim and the fire (21:68–69), the unmatched kingdom (38:35) and "kept saying thanks"

### Takeaway
The Qarun and Ibrahim lines are accurate. In 38:35 the kingdom is a dua; the Quran shows it answered (38:36–39), and the Prophet ﷺ treated it as Sulaiman's exclusive gift in Bukhari 3423. "A kingdom no one will ever have again" is therefore defensible, but "a kingdom such as no one after him would have" mirrors the verse. "Kept saying: thanks" is a fair summary, not a quotation.

### Cited Findings
- SI 28:78: "I was only given it because of knowledge I have." — [corpus.quran.com 28:78](https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=28&verse=78), [quran.com 28:78](https://quran.com/28:78)
- 28:81 "And We caused the earth to swallow him and his home" (standard SI, not re-fetched) — [quran.com 28:78-82](https://quran.com/il-racconto/78-82)
- Bukhari 3423 / 461 / 4808: the Prophet ﷺ caught an ifrit and meant to tie it to a mosque pillar, "but I remembered the invocation of my brother Solomon: 'And grant me a kingdom such as shall not belong to any other after me' (38:35)", so he let it go — [sunnah.com bukhari:3423](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3423), [sunnah.com bukhari:461](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:461), [sunnah.com bukhari:4808](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4808)
- 38:35–39 (the dua, then "So We subjected to him the wind... This is Our gift") and 21:68–69 ("O fire, be coolness and safety upon Abraham") — standard SI, not re-fetched: [quran.com 38:35](https://quran.com/38/35), [quran.com 21:69](https://quran.com/21/69). The project's own Ibrahim upload text quotes 21:69 identically (long/03-ibrahim/UPLOAD.md).
- 27:19 (Sulaiman's dua "My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favor...") and 34:13 ("Work, O family of David, in gratitude") support the gratitude theme — [quran.com 27:19](https://quran.com/27/19), [quran.com 34:13](https://quran.com/34/13)

### Inferences
- "Same test, two answers" (Qarun 28:78 vs Sulaiman 27:40) is a sound narrator contrast. "Far more" is inference, not text.

### Gaps
- Bukhari wording was confirmed from sunnah.com search excerpts only; the pages could not be opened.

## Q4: Anything added beyond the verses?

### Takeaway
There is no invented story content and no Israiliyat (no names for the one with knowledge, no durations, no numbers). The additions are three small embellishments: "deep" water, the palace "over water", and the jinn "could carry" a throne. There is also one wording shift: "a man… from the Book".

### Cited Findings
- 27:44 says "a body of water" with no depth, and nothing about water under the glass — [corpus.quran.com 27:44](https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=27&verse=44)
- 27:39–40: the jinn offered; the one with knowledge brought the throne — [legacy.quran.com 27:40](https://legacy.quran.com/27/40)

### Inferences
- With the five fixes listed in the summary, all of ch09–12 meets the checklist rule "Only what the Quran / sahih sources say".

### Gaps
- None beyond the network limits noted at the top.
