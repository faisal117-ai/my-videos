# Sulaiman (AS) film, chapters 5–8: source check (Quran 27:20–42)

**Verification caveat:** the session's network blocked quran.com, api.quran.com, alquran.cloud and jsDelivr, so I could not fetch the text live. The Sahih International wording below is quoted from memory of that translation. Each verse link is given so it can be spot-checked before the voice lock. No line in ch05–08 relies on hadith or on tafsir-only detail.

## Line-by-line verdicts (ch05–ch08)

### Takeaway
All 25 lines are either backed by Surah an-Naml 27:20–42 (with 27:16, 34:15 and 41:37 for context) or are rhetorical narrator lines. **Nothing is NOT IN SOURCE.** The fixes are wording only: 5.3 and 7.2 are close to Sahih International but not word-for-word, and 7.4 adds two small inferences ("she was wise", "she did not want war").

### Inferences
- The chapters stick closely to 27:20–42. No Isra'iliyat content was found: no gift inventory, no hoopoe dropping the letter in her lap, no reason for the hoopoe's absence such as finding water, and no retinue numbers.
- "A rich land. A great throne. A strong army." is supported (27:23, 34:15, 27:33).
- "A whole people, bowing to the sun" is supported (27:24, 27:43).
