# Sulaiman (AS) film: source check of narration ch00–ch04

**Method and limit:** this session's network blocks quran.com and api.quran.com, so the verses could not be fetched live. The Sahih International (SI) wording below is quoted from the standard SI text as I know it. Before the film is published, check each quote against quran.com: open `https://quran.com/<surah>:<ayah>` and select the Sahih International translation. Only the Quran and sahih hadith count as sources here, and no tafsir narration is used. Line ids are chapter.line, numbered from 0.

## Line-by-line verdicts

### Takeaway
All 31 lines are either supported by the Quran or plainly rhetorical. Nothing comes from Israiliyat or tafsir alone, and the narration adds no numbers or places. Four lines need small wording fixes, all flagged (minor) below: 2.5 (the Quran says "some of the jinn"), 3.2 ("later in this story" points to Qarun, who is not part of Sulaiman's story), 3.4 ("smallest creature") and 4.5 ("stopped for an ant"). The quotes in 4.1 and 4.6 differ slightly from SI wording.

### Inferences
- Key question "Dawud, the king of Bani Israil": supported. 2:246 places the story among the Children of Israel and their request for a king. 2:251 says Allah gave Dawud "the kingship and prophethood", and 38:20 says "We strengthened his kingdom".
- Every key verse listed in the brief (21:78–79, 27:16–19, 34:12–13, 38:35–36) is quoted or paraphrased faithfully. There are no added numbers or place names. The film never says what the judgement in the sheep case was, which is the tafsir-only part, so that is correctly avoided.
- The only additions beyond the verses are "smallest creature" (3.4), "stopped" (4.5), "the jinn" rather than "some of the jinn" (2.5), and "later in this story" pointing to Qarun (3.2). Each is a one-word or one-phrase fix.
