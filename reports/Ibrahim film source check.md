# Ibrahim film needs twenty lines replaced

The Prophet Ibrahim (AS) film narration (ch00–ch14, **109 lines**) follows the Quran's own story closely. The verse quotes from 6:74–79, 19:42–47, 21:52–70, 37:99–107, 2:258–260, 2:127–129, 14:35–41 and 11:69–73 are accurate to Sahih International (SI). The film's best-known set pieces, however, are mostly not from the Quran or sahih hadith. **20 lines carry a claim that is only in tafsir, Isra'iliyat, popular lore or the narrator's imagination.** These include the birds falling from the sky (0.0), the festival and the temple (4.3), wood gathered for days and a fire no one could approach (5.2), the burned ropes (5.5), the pardon-or-kill demonstration (6.3), Lut (AS) as "nephew" (7.1), Zamzam bursting "at the baby's feet" (8.5), and "the dreams of prophets are revelation" presented as if sourced (10.1). Two lines contradict a sahih hadith outright. Line 9.0 says Ibrahim (AS) saw the water rise, but in Bukhari 3364 he had already left when Zamzam appeared. Line 14.1 says "every time, he was alone", but Ismail (AS) built the Kaaba with him ([Quran 2:127](https://quran.com/2/127)). **23 more lines are sourced but need a wording fix**: a detail the verse does not give, a misquote of SI, or the house closing line. The remaining 66 are OK. Every flagged line has a Quran/sahih-only replacement below. The 5.5 replacement is built so that "Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakeel" ([Bukhari 4563](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4563)) becomes the film's dua thread, from the fire to the closing lesson. **Limit:** sunnah.com and every other hadith host were blocked in this session. All hadith wording comes from search excerpts and must be checked once before the voice lock (last section).

## Hadith wording and four claims stayed unverified

Every Quran quote was checked word for word against the local SI file. The only exceptions are 14:39 and 19:46–48, which were cited but not re-read there. The hadith side is weaker. **sunnah.com, alim.org, sacred-texts.com, cdn.jsdelivr.net and api.hadith.gading.dev were all blocked by this session's network.** The numbers and wording of Bukhari 138, 3350, 3357, 3358, 3359, 3364, 3365, 3370, 3372, 4563 and 4564, and Muslim 406 and 2276, come only from search excerpts that agree with one another. Muslim 2276 is cited from memory. Four specific points remain open. First, the exact Bukhari 3364 wording ("with his heel (or his wing)", "will not neglect us", the sa'y sentence) needs checking. Second, no Bukhari or Muslim text was found that places the salawat explicitly inside salah, so 14.3's "every time we pray" rests on established practice. Third, nobody checked whether an Ibn 'Abbas "dreams of prophets are revelation" narration exists in the Sahihayn; the excerpts found point only to other collections ([al-Matalib al-'Aliya 3398](https://hadithunlocked.com/matalib:3398)). Fourth, the original isnad of the birds report was not traced. That one does not change the verdict, because the report is absent from the Quran and both Sahihs either way.

## Conclusion

Each of the 20 not-in-source lines replaces a quieter verse with a more cinematic detail: birds, a festival, ropes, a catapult, a kicking baby, a tent. The same thing happened in the Sulaiman film, and here it went one step further. Two of these details contradict a sahih hadith rather than just going beyond it. For future scripts, the most useful check is to ask of each vivid detail which verse or hadith number it comes from, before it reaches the storyboard. The Quranic text supplies enough drama on its own: "I am ill" in place of the festival, a furnace, and "Allah is enough for us" spoken in the fire. Allowing sunnah.com in the environment for one pass closes the last verification gap before the voice lock.
