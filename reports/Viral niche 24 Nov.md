## The one shared structure
The Short speaks to the viewer's own heart: one verse or one miracle that answers a fear the viewer already has
(hardship, sin, loneliness, loss), said to "you". Comfort first, story second. Our biggest Short and the niche's
best faceless Shorts are all this; our plain story recaps sit near the median.
