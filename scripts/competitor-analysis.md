## What the data says

### 1. The big channels were built on long videos, not Shorts
Of the top four, Shorts are 0–2% of views (One Islam: 16%, almost all from one series). Stories of
the Prophets reached 1M subscribers with **102 videos**; Deen Stories TV reached 199K with **17**.
Shorts in this niche get 4K–12K typical views even on million-subscriber channels.

### 2. The winning video is the complete story of one prophet
- Stories of the Prophets: Nuh 12M, Ibrahim 8.1M, Muhammad ﷺ 7.7M, Musa 7.2M, Adam 5.1M.
- IQRA Cartoon: Adam 6.3M, Isa 5.3M, Musa 4.2M, Ibrahim 2.5M.
- Islam With Idriss: Musa 1.1M, Muhammad ﷺ 731K, Ibrahim 596K.
Musa and Ibrahim are in the top five on every story channel.

### 4. Their first videos were their biggest hits

videos get far less (Stories of the Prophets: 1.1M typical early, 186K latest; IQRA 447K → 110K):
the plain prophet-story search is now crowded.

- One Islam: "The Muslim Undertaker" series, 82 Shorts, 41M views (11M top).

## Where Haq Stories stands
- **Shorts are working for a channel of this size:** 55 Shorts, 25K views, "Ease with every hardship" 3K.
- **Long videos are not being found yet:** the Yusuf film has 17 views. Nothing sends viewers to it:
  it has no search history yet, and only the Yusuf Shorts (via Related video, once set) point to it.
- Long videos are 2 out of 57 uploads; in the league they are almost everything.

## The plan to reach their league

### 1. Make the long film the main product (weekly)
- **"Stories of the Prophets" series, 20–30 min each**, one per week, in a fixed format and voice.
- Order: Musa (Mon 16 Nov) → **Ibrahim** → **Nuh** → **Adam** → Isa → Sulaiman → Yunus → Ayyub → Lut,
  Hud, Saleh → Dawud. The most-watched stories in the niche come first.
- **Fixed search title template**, prophet name first:
  `The Complete Story of Prophet Ibrahim (AS) | Stories of the Prophets | Haq Stories`
- One playlist, "Stories of the Prophets", every film in order.

### 2. Every second week, add one unseen-world film (20–40 min)
From the Quran and sahih hadith only, told through scenes and silhouettes, never showing angels:
Gog and Magog (Al-Kahf 18:94–98), the Grave and Barzakh, the Day of Judgment, Jannah, Jahannam,
Dajjal, Queen Bilqis and Sulaiman (An-Naml 27). This is where the newer channels are growing.

### 3. After 8–10 films: one feature-length compilation
"The 25 Prophets in the Quran" (60–90 min), joining the films into one movie with new links and a new
intro. It is a top video on three channels. (Join our own films with new narration between them,
so it is a new work, not a re-upload.)

### 4. Shorts change role: they feed the films
- Keep about 30 s, the hit formula, and **every Short points to a film** (Related video).
- Each film gets 3–5 Shorts written fresh from its strongest moments ("Pharaoh's last words…",
  "The man Allah stopped the sun for…").
- Consider **1 Short a day** instead of 2, and spend the time on the weekly film: in this niche the
  films carry the channels.

### 5. Packaging the league uses
- Titles: the searched words first (prophet's name, "Complete Story", "Full Movie").
- 20+ min films with chapters, a mid-video link to the next film, an end screen to the next one.

## What it means for monetisation
4,000 watch hours ≈ one 25-min film with 32,000 views watched for 30% (about 7.5 min each).
Islam With Idriss, 60K subscribers, got 1.1M views on its Musa film. **One film that catches on in
search clears the hours on its own;** a library of 20+ films makes that very likely.
