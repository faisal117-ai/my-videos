# Miracle of Islam (@miracleofislamm): detailed research (17 Nov 2026)

The channel that inspired Haq Stories. Read from public data only: the channel pages, its RSS feed, all 96
visible Shorts (date, views, likes, description) and the 30 newest long videos, plus thumbnails and sample
frames. Watch pages, analytics and comments text were not available.

## 1. The channel in numbers
- **218K subscribers · 11.95M views · 151 videos** (96 Shorts, ~55 long). English. Country: United States.
  Joined 27 Jan 2023, but **every visible video is from March 2026 or later**: older uploads were removed or
  the channel was repurposed. Its real growth happened in 2026.
- Engagement on Shorts is strong: **likes ≈ 7.5% of views** (median), ~20–30 comments per Short.

## 2. The timeline: how it grew and how it slowed
| Period | What they posted | Typical views |
|---|---|---|
| **Mar–Apr 2026** | **60 Shorts in 2 months (about 1 a day)** | median **61K–77K**, hits 0.5–1.1M |
| May–Jun 2026 | nothing (2-month gap) | — |
| Jul–Sep 2026 | 34 Shorts | median **~11K**, best 80K |
| Oct 2026 | 2 Shorts; switch to long videos | Shorts ~2.5K |
| 20 Oct – 11 Nov | **a long video every 1–2 days** (11–17 min, plus 52-min and 1-hour compilations) | median **~7K**, best 33K |

**Lesson 1:** the daily burst of mercy and prophet Shorts built the channel in two months.
**Lesson 2:** after a 2-month gap, Shorts never came back (views ~6× lower). Consistency matters more than any trick.

## 4. How their Shorts are made
- **Look:** photorealistic AI images, cinematic and warm (desert gold, oil lamps, shafts of light), slow
  camera moves. Recent Shorts show prophets and Companions as human figures with a glowing white face.
- **Captions:** one or two words at a time, big, centred mid-screen, yellow-green highlight.
- **Voice:** one English male narrator.
- **Titles:** 2–6 words, often a question, sometimes 😱/😢/😨. **Descriptions are empty.**
- **Topics:** mercy, repentance, charity, Paradise, prophets, Companions (Umar, Ali, Uwais, Abu Lubaba), and
  stories of ordinary people (the thief, the poor man, the three travellers, two brothers).

## 5. How their long videos are made (Oct–Nov 2026)
- **Mostly invented stories told as real news:** named characters, dates and places ("On 14 October 2026, the
  Saudi Interior Ministry announced the execution…", "3 minutes ago in Makkah… millions of white worms",
  "Christian Pilots Burn QURAN at Atlanta Airport").
- **Thumbnails:** photorealistic AI scenes, two-line headline bars (white/red/yellow), red circles and arrows,
  fake "BREAKING NEWS" and "LIVE" badges; one uses a real Saudi ruler's face.
- **Titles:** fear and shock ("TERRIFIED the Entire World", "Story will make you scream", "never ignore this
  video… otherwise you will regret it").
- **Risk:** YouTube's misinformation, misleading-metadata and impersonation policies; it also spends viewers'
  trust. Even so, these get only 1–33K views.

## 6. What Haq Stories adopts (see shorts-plan.md)
1. **A daily rhythm with no gaps**, plus batches made ahead so a gap can never happen.
2. **Their topic areas,** only with a Quran or sahih source, in our own script and scenes.
3. **Short question titles**, story questions only: the 50-50 test from 23 Nov.
4. **Big word-by-word captions** in our Shorts (karaoke style, centred), to test against our current text cards.
5. **Ordinary-people stories from hadith** (the three men in the cave; the leper, bald and blind men; the
   1,000 dinars), not only prophets.
6. **Evergreen question films** people search for, as our Quran-wonders films.
7. **Compilations of our own work:** e.g. "1 Hour of Stories of the Prophets", "Stories for sleep". They
   earn long-video watch hours toward the 4,000, from content we already have.
8. **Thumbnail layout that gets clicks:** one bold two-line headline and one arrow or circle on the key object,
   but honest words, no fake news badges.

## 7. What Haq Stories never adopts
- Showing prophets, the Prophet ﷺ, Companions or righteous women in any form (even with a glowing face).
- Invented stories, invented "news", real people's faces, "BREAKING NEWS"/"LIVE" badges, fear-bait titles.
- Their stories without a reliable source (Musa's neighbour in Paradise, Salahuddin and the Christian girl…).
- Photorealistic AI people: our stylised animation keeps the no-faces rule and the "altered or synthetic"
  answer simple, and it was our best-performing Short style (November: 4 of 4 pushed).

## 8. Where Haq Stories can win
- **Trust:** every verse and hadith sourced on screen, nothing invented.
- **Urdu/Hindi:** they have none; a large audience they do not serve.
- **Series:** numbered films and arcs that keep viewers watching the next one; their videos are one-offs.
- **Consistency:** they faltered after a gap; daily Shorts plus weekly films, every week, beats them over time.
