# Long video 1: The Complete Story of Prophet Yusuf (AS)

**Working title:** From the Well to the Throne: The Complete Story of Prophet Yusuf (AS) | Surah Yusuf
**Length:** 11–13 minutes · **Format:** 16:9 landscape, 1920×1080 · **Narration:** English (Callum), Urdu subtitles
**Source:** Surah Yusuf (12), told in order, every event cited by verse

## Requirements this video must meet

### YouTube monetisation
- **Watch hours** only count from long videos like this one. Shorts don't count toward the 4,000.
- **Original content, not stitched Shorts.** YouTube refuses monetisation for "reused" or mass-produced
  content. This video gets its own full script, new narration and new landscape scenes. Nothing is
  copied from the Shorts except the visual style.
- **Chapters** (timestamps in the description) for watch time and search.
- **A custom thumbnail** (long videos need one; Shorts don't).
- **The altered or synthetic content setting:** "No". The animation is clearly stylised, not realistic,
  and the voice doesn't imitate a real person. Re-check YouTube's rule when uploading.
- **Rights:** ElevenLabs Creator plan (commercial use allowed), our own sounds, open-licence fonts,
  short cited verse quotes.

### Channel rules (shorts/README.md)
- **Never shown:** Yusuf (AS), Yaqub (AS), any prophet or Companion, in any form (no figure,
  silhouette or light standing in for them). Their words and actions come only through voice and text.
- **Never shown:** the women of the story (the wife of al-Aziz, the women of the city), angels and jinn.
  Their scenes are told through objects and places: closed doors, a torn shirt, knives and fruit.
- **The brothers:** only as distant, faceless shadows. **Others** (the caravan, al-Aziz, the king,
  guards, prisoners): faceless silhouettes.
- **Arabic** from the verified Quran package, never typed by hand. **English:** Sahih International.
  **Urdu:** Maududi.
- **Honorifics:** "Hazrat ___ (AS)" on screen; spoken "alayhis salaam".
- **Sound:** a story soundscape plus voices-only humming, no instruments, mixed with `tools/mix.py`.
- **Every chapter gets its own scene.**

### Retention (what we learned from the Shorts)
- **The first 60 seconds are the hook:** start at the climax, then go back.
- **A new chapter every 60–90 seconds,** each ending on a small cliffhanger.
- **Movement and sound** at every scene change; no static screens.

## Production steps
1. **Script:** I write the full narration (about 1,700 words) with verse citations. **You approve it before any voice is generated** (saves credits).
2. **Voice:** Callum, one take per chapter, about 11,000 credits of your 100,000.
3. **Scenes:** a landscape version of the template plus new scenes (palace, caravan, doors, prison, cows, granaries, home). Reuse the well and dream scenes.
4. **Sound:** mostly the existing library; a few new sounds (caravan, prison, cows, palace).
5. **Check and stills per chapter,** then render (about 30–60 minutes for 12 minutes of video).
6. **Package:** title, description with chapters, tags, thumbnail.
