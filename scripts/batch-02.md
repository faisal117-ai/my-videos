# Haq Stories: Batch 02 (5 Shorts, for approval)

Written to the hit formula in `channel-plan.md`: one story, a surprising first line, hope or mercy
at the end, **35–40 seconds** (about 90–100 spoken words), title "X… then Y" + one emoji.
English narration (Callum, ElevenLabs), Urdu subtitles, 1080×1920, animated Haq Stories style.
Every Short ends: "A new story from the Quran, every day. Follow."

**House rules (unchanged):** Arabic and Sahih International English from the `quran-json`
package, never typed. Urdu: Maududi. No prophet shown in any form; righteous women never shown;
others only as faceless silhouettes. Soundscape + voices-only humming, no instruments.

**Posting slots:** 11:00 and 18:30 Pakistan time.

## 1. The man who died for 100 years

**Title:** Allah made him die for 100 years… and his food hadn't even spoiled 🍇
**Source:** Al-Baqarah 2:259 · **Scene:** a ruined town, a basket of food and a water skin, a donkey's bones
**Note:** the Quran doesn't name him (tradition says Uzair), so we don't either, and he isn't shown.

[ON SCREEN: a ruined town at dusk, "Allah made him die for 100 years…"]
A man passed by a town lying in ruins. He wondered: how could Allah ever bring this back to life?
[ON SCREEN: the sky turning day-night-day fast, a counter climbing to "100", "2:259"]
So Allah caused him to die, for one hundred years. Then He brought him back.
[ON SCREEN: "How long have you remained?"]
He was asked: how long did you stay? He said: a day, or part of a day.
[ON SCREEN: the food and drink, fresh; the donkey's bones beside them]
One hundred years. Yet his food and his drink had not changed at all. But his donkey was only bones.
[ON SCREEN: bones rising and being covered, verse 2:259 "I know that Allah is over all things competent."]
Then he watched Allah raise the bones, and cover them with flesh. And he said: I know that Allah is over all things competent.
A new story from the Quran, every day. Follow.

About 40 seconds.

## 2. Musa's (AS) mother and the river

**Title:** She put her baby in the river… and Allah promised to bring him back 🌊
**Source:** Al-Qasas 28:7–13 · **Scene:** the Nile at night, a small basket on the water, a palace bank
**Never shown:** the mother, the sister, the wife of Pharaoh, Musa (AS). Only the river, the basket and the palace.

[ON SCREEN: a basket on a dark river, "She put her baby in the river…"]
Pharaoh was killing the baby boys of Bani Israel. And one mother was terrified for her son.
[ON SCREEN: verse 28:7, "cast him into the river and do not fear and do not grieve"]
Then Allah inspired her: when you fear for him, cast him into the river, and do not fear, and do not grieve.
[ON SCREEN: the basket drifting toward the palace bank]
The river carried him straight to the palace of the very man who wanted him dead.
[ON SCREEN: an empty cradle at home, "28:10"]
Her heart became empty. But the baby refused every nurse in the palace.
[ON SCREEN: the basket returned to a lit doorway, verse 28:13 "that she would know that the promise of Allah is true"]
So Allah returned him to his mother, so that she would know: the promise of Allah is true.
A new story from the Quran, every day. Follow.

About 40 seconds.

## 3. Yunus (AS) in the darkness

**Title:** Swallowed in the darkness of the sea… then he said one sentence 🐋
**Source:** Al-Anbiya 21:87–88 (the fish swallowing him: As-Saffat 37:142) · **Scene:** a storm at sea, a ship, the great fish, layers of darkness, then dawn on a shore
**Note:** the Quran says "the fish". The title's 🐋 is the familiar picture; narration says "a huge fish".

[ON SCREEN: a deep, dark ocean, "Swallowed in the darkness…"]
Hazrat Yunus, alayhis salaam, left his people in anger, before Allah gave him permission.
[ON SCREEN: a ship in a storm, then a huge fish beneath it, "37:142"]
He boarded a ship. A storm came. He was thrown into the sea, and a huge fish swallowed him.
[ON SCREEN: three layers of darkness: night, sea, the fish, "21:87"]
Darkness of the night. Darkness of the sea. Darkness inside the fish.
[ON SCREEN: verse 21:87, Arabic + "There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers."]
And from inside that darkness, he called out: There is no god but You. Glory be to You. I was among the wrongdoers.
[ON SCREEN: light breaking, a shore at dawn, verse 21:88 "And thus do We save the believers."]
And Allah answered him, and saved him. And Allah says: this is how We save the believers.
A new story from the Quran, every day. Follow.

About 40 seconds.

## 4. The four birds (remake of the 78%-watched Short)

**Title:** Four birds were cut into pieces… then they flew back alive 🕊️
**Source:** Al-Baqarah 2:260 · **Scene:** four hills at dawn, four birds, feathers, wind
**Note:** the slaughter is shown only as scattered feathers on four hills, never as blood.

[ON SCREEN: four birds against the dawn, "Four birds were cut into pieces…"]
Hazrat Ibrahim, alayhis salaam, asked Allah: My Lord, show me how You give life to the dead.
[ON SCREEN: "Have you not believed?" then "Yes, but only that my heart may be satisfied."]
Allah asked: Have you not believed? He said: Yes. But I ask so that my heart may be at peace.
[ON SCREEN: four birds, then feathers scattered on four distant hills, "2:260"]
So Allah told him: take four birds, cut them into pieces, and put a part of them on each hill.
[ON SCREEN: a single call echoing across the hills]
Then call them.
[ON SCREEN: feathers gathering, four birds flying in fast, verse 2:260 "they will come [flying] to you in haste"]
And they came flying back to him, alive, in haste. Know that Allah is Exalted in Might, and Wise.
A new story from the Quran, every day. Follow.

About 37 seconds.

## 5. Ayyub (AS)

**Title:** He lost his health, his family, everything… and said only this 🤲
**Source:** Al-Anbiya 21:83–84 (his illness and patience: Sad 38:41–44) · **Scene:** an empty home, a withered field, rain, then green fields and a spring
**Note:** the details of what he lost (wealth, children, years of illness) are from tafsir; the narration keeps to what the verses say.

[ON SCREEN: an empty home, a dry field, "He lost everything…"]
Hazrat Ayyub, alayhis salaam, was tested with everything: his health, his family, his wealth.
[ON SCREEN: years passing over the same empty home, "38:41"]
For years, the hardship stayed. And he never complained about Allah, once.
[ON SCREEN: verse 21:83, Arabic + "Indeed, adversity has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful."]
He only said: Adversity has touched me. And You are the Most Merciful of the merciful.
[ON SCREEN: a spring bursting from the ground, "38:42"]
That was all. And Allah answered him.
[ON SCREEN: green fields, a full home lit at night, verse 21:84 "We gave him [back] his family and the like thereof with them as mercy from Us"]
He took away his pain, and gave him back his family, and as many again with them, as a mercy.
A new story from the Quran, every day. Follow.

About 38 seconds.
