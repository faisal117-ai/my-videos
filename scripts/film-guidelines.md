# Long-form (film) guidelines: user's rules, 23 Nov 2026

Shorts run on swipe-away; long-form runs on **CTR × AVD × session contribution**. A 12 % CTR with 20 % AVD loses to a
6 % CTR with 55 % retention. The Shorts hook rules (frame-0 text, spoken hook ≤ 4 s, scorecard /100) do NOT apply to films.

## Core: session contribution
YouTube's dominant 2026 signal. Films that keep viewers on YouTube afterwards get more Suggested placements. Build the
chain: end screens, playlists, series, "watch next".

## Rule 1: Packaging (title + thumbnail) earns the click
- CTR 4–10 % healthy, ~6 % average; < 3 % for a week = packaging problem.
- Thumbnail: ONE clear subject (64 % higher CTR than cluttered). Max 4 elements, ideally 3 or fewer. Nothing in the
  bottom-right corner (timestamp). Contrasting colours from opposite sides of the wheel.
- Title formulas: curiosity gap, number/listicle, mistake/negativity, versus/comparison.
- Never repeat thumbnail text in the title; together they tell the story and spark curiosity.

## Rule 2: The first 30 seconds decide everything
- No intro before the hook: no logo sting, no "welcome back", no trailer. Open mid-action on the promise.
- Restate the title's promise in our own voice, then raise the stakes to open a loop.
- Complete the hook by 0:30; keep 70 %+ there. A drop at 0:15–0:30 = the open over-promised or wandered → re-cut the
  first lines before touching anything later.

## Rule 3: Story architecture (moral epic)
- Setup 0:00–0:30: hook creates tension; restate the promise.
- Build 0:30–~60 %: one story, relentless pacing, complications, rising stakes.
- Climax / revelation ~60–75 %: the twist or moral pivot.
- Explicit lesson, final 15–25 %: the takeaway, with tafsir / hadith context and application.
- Stronger opening hook (curiosity or urgency before substance). One pillar (Islamic faith); build 5–10 story
  templates around it; do not mix pillars across uploads.

## Rule 4: Retention mechanics mid-video
- Pattern interrupt every 45–90 s (major: B-roll burst, style change, sound shift; minor: on-screen text, cut).
- Re-hook at 2:00 and every 3–4 min after.
- First major payoff before 2:00 (a down-payment on the hook's promise before the 2–3 min valley).
- Open loops early, resolve late: raise a question in the first 60 s and hold the answer.
- Never a mid-video "smash subscribe". If needed, a brief CTA at ~33 %, 20 s max.

## Rule 5: Length and cadence
- 8–15 min optimal when retention holds; never pad. Cutting length 30–40 % is often the highest-leverage fix.
- Retention targets: 5–10 min → 45 %+ good, 60 %+ excellent; 10–20 min → 40 %+ good, 55 %+ excellent.
- ~1–2 long-form per week, consistently; gaps cause deprioritisation. Healthy mix: ~1 long-form + 3–5 Shorts per week.

## Rule 6: Accuracy and trust (even more critical long-form)
- Every hadith / verse reference verifiable (Bukhari number, verse number).
- Label Isra'iliyyat or weak traditions clearly, or cut them.
- Never fabricate quotes or attribute words to prophets / Companions without a source.
- AI video retains less than human-fronted; TTS must sound natural with no artefacts (AI narration can trigger a
  drop-off in the first 45 s).

## Rule 7: Measure these, not a scorer (Friday review)
| Metric | Target | Tells you |
|---|---|---|
| CTR | 4–10 % (6 % avg); < 3 % = packaging problem | Does the thumbnail/title earn the click? |
| AVD (% viewed) | 40–60 % by length | Does the film deliver the promise? |
| First-30 s retention | 70 %+ | Did the open survive the cliff? |
| Session contribution | viewers keep watching after | Will YouTube recommend it further? |
| Returning viewers / subs from video | rising trend | Is it building loyalty? |

High CTR + low AVD = misleading packaging → demoted. Low CTR + high AVD = good film, weak packaging → fix packaging first.

## Bottom line
1. Package honestly: title and thumbnail make one promise, no repetition.
2. Survive the 30-second cliff: no intro, open on the promise, hook complete by 0:30.
3. One story, relentless pacing: interrupt every 45–90 s, re-hook every 2–3 min.
4. 8–15 min if retention holds; never pad.
5. Protect trust: every claim sourced, every tradition labelled.
6. Build the chain: end screens, series, playlists.
7. One long-form per week, consistently, with 3–5 Shorts for discovery.

## Pre-publish film check (run on every film; Rule 7 metrics are judged after, on Friday)
- [ ] Title: one promise, a formula from Rule 1, no thumbnail words repeated; series tag after the hook.
- [ ] Thumbnail: one subject, ≤ 4 elements, nothing bottom-right, contrasting colours, correct EP number.
- [ ] 0:00–0:30: no intro, opens on the promise, restates it, opens a loop, hook complete by 0:30.
- [ ] First payoff before 2:00; re-hook lines at ~2:00 and every 3–4 min; a question in the first 60 s held late.
- [ ] Pattern interrupt (scene change / text / sound) every 45–90 s; no mid-video subscribe ask.
- [ ] Structure: build to ~60 %, climax 60–75 %, explicit lesson in the final 15–25 %.
- [ ] Length 8–15 min; nothing padded.
- [ ] Every claim sourced; no Isra'iliyyat unlabelled; no invented quotes. Arabic voiced with eleven_v3 (voice.md).

## Film CTA policy (user approved 24 Nov 2026)
| When | CTA |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | None (no subscribe, no channel intro) |
| ~33 % (≈ 5 min) | Silent on-screen chip for 4 s: "Subscribe · a new prophet every week" (no voice) |
| Mid-film | Nothing else; never a spoken "subscribe" |
| Last spoken line | One ask: a heartfelt comment question + "next: the full movie of Prophet …" |
| Last 20 s | End screen: the next film (big) + Subscribe button |
| Description / pinned comment | First line film links; pinned comment = the same question |
