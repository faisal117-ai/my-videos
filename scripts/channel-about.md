# Channel "About" text (24 Nov 2026, for Studio → Customisation → Basic info → Description)

Haq Stories | حق کہانیاں brings the stories of the prophets and the verses of the Quran to life, in English and Urdu/Hindi.

Every story is researched from the Quran and authentic (sahih) hadith only, checked verse by verse, with the reference shown on screen. Each verse appears in Arabic with its English (Sahih International) and Urdu (Maulana Maududi) translation. Anything without a sound source is left out.

No prophet, Companion, righteous woman, angel or jinn is ever shown in any form. The illustrations and narration are made with AI tools; the research, scripts and checking are done by us.

New every week:
• Daily Shorts: a verse for your heart, and a moment from the stories of the prophets
• Weekend films: Stories of the Prophets (English) · Qasas ul Anbiya (Urdu/Hindi)
