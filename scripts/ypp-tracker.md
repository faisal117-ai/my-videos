# Monetization (YPP) tracker: updated every Friday review

Targets (rules from 1 Feb 2027): **1,000 subscribers** and **4,000 public watch hours in 12 months**
(or 4M valid public Shorts views in 90 days). Watch hours from paid ads and from Shorts do NOT count:
only people who choose to watch a long video. So the plan is: Shorts → films (watch hours), better film retention.

Where the numbers come from each Friday:
- Subscribers: Metricool (YTEV01) or Studio.
- Public watch hours + valid Shorts views (90 days): Studio → **Earn** tab (user sends a screenshot on Friday).
- Film average view duration: Studio → each film → Engagement (needed to see if retention fixes work).
