## Notes for your review

1. **Length:** about 2,170 spoken words, **13:57** of voice (chapter times above are from the generated takes). Trimmed from the first draft: the "kill him" debate, the wording of the prisoners' dreams, the women's testimony, the returned payment, the oath and the eldest brother staying behind, the brothers asking forgiveness, and the closing prayer of 12:101.
2. **The hook doesn't show the brothers kneeling.** The plan said "kneel", but in 12:58 and 12:88 they only stand and ask for grain; the bowing is in 12:100. The hook combines 12:58 (he knows them, they don't know him) with 12:88 (their plea), which happens on the last visit.
3. **Not from the Quran, flagged so you can decide:**
   - The name **Binyamin**, which comes from tradition; the Quran says only "his brother". It's said once in chapter 9 and shown with "known as" on screen.
   - "He did not say 'when He took me out of the well'" (chapter 12) is a well-known tafsir observation (Ibn Kathir and others) on 12:100, not a statement in the verse. It's worded as our reflection, not as Quran.
4. **The three shirts** (false blood → torn from the back → healing) and **"patience is most fitting" said twice** (12:18, 12:83) are the threads that carry the viewer across chapters. Each chapter ends on a small cliffhanger, as the plan asks.
5. **Kept careful on purpose:** 12:24 is only "he saw the proof of his Lord; Allah turned evil away from him". The wife of al-Aziz isn't named (the name Zulaikha isn't in the Quran). No fruit is mentioned in the narration (the verse says "banquet"); the fruit only appears in the scene.
6. **Words to check in `tools/tts.py` OVERRIDES before generating:** Yusuf, Yaqub, Ishaq, Ibrahim, Binyamin, al-Aziz / Aziz, alayhis salaam, Sabrun jameel, dirhams. (Callum is ElevenLabs, so this may mean a pronunciation dictionary or spelled-out phonetics there instead.)
