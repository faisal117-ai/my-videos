## The hit formula
> A Quran or sahih story · a surprising detail in the first line · hope or mercy at the end ·
> **about 30 s** (never over 35) · animated Haq Stories style · title "X… then Y" + one emoji ·
> say the hook once, never repeat it · end on a 1-second "Follow for more." so the Short loops

No more advice Shorts, no more "Part 1/2" series Shorts, no Urdu duplicates, and no Shorts cut straight
from a long video without rewriting them ("Never lost hope", 56 s, held 28% on 13 Nov: about 16 s watched).

## Monetisation path (Route B: Shorts bring subscribers, long videos bring watch hours)
Check YouTube Studio → Earn for the current thresholds.

| Level | Needs |
|---|---|
| 1: fan funding | 500 subs + 3 uploads in 90 days + 3,000 long-video hours in 12 months (or 3M Shorts views in 90 days) |
| 2: ad revenue | 1,000 subs + 4,000 long-video hours in 12 months (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days) |

- **Phase 1 (0 → 500 subs):** one Short a day to the formula; a long video every 1–2 weeks with 3 Shorts cut from it, linked by Related video.
- **Phase 2 (500 → 1,000 subs + 4,000 hours):** a long video every week, "The Complete Story of…": **Musa (AS)**, Ibrahim (AS), Yunus (AS), Nuh (AS), Ayyub (AS). 4,000 hours ≈ 60,000 views of a 14-minute video watched for 4 minutes.
- **Projection:** ~1,265 views a day × 30 ≈ 38,000 views a month ≈ 190 subs a month, so 1,000 subs in about 4–5 months at best; 6–12 months for full monetisation.

## Rules that protect monetisation
- Every video is a different story with its own script and scene; no re-uploads; nothing from other channels.
- Altered or synthetic content: "No" while the animation stays stylised and the voice imitates no real person.
- Never buy views or subscribers.
