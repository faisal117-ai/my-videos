## The hit formula
> A Quran or sahih story · a surprising detail in the first line · hope or mercy at the end ·
> **about 30 s** (never over 35) · animated Haq Stories style · title "X… then Y" + one emoji ·
> say the hook once, never repeat it · end on a 1-second "Follow for more." so the Short loops

No more advice Shorts, no more "Part 1/2" series Shorts, no Urdu duplicates, and no Shorts cut straight
from a long video without rewriting them ("Never lost hope", 56 s, held 28% on 13 Nov: about 16 s watched).

## Rules that protect monetisation
- Every video is a different story with its own script and scene; no re-uploads; nothing from other channels.
- Altered or synthetic content: "No" while the animation stays stylised and the voice imitates no real person.
- Never buy views or subscribers.

## Routine
- **Long films: English every Saturday evening, the Urdu version every Sunday evening, at 6:30 PM PKT** — weekends, when viewers have time. Every film is made in both languages. On film days the evening slot is the film's alone: no evening Short that day (decided 14 Nov).
- **Daily: two uploads, at 11:00 and 18:30 Pakistan time (Asia/Karachi)**, scheduled through Metricool. Mostly Shorts; a long video takes one of the two slots on its day. Reply to every comment in the first hour after each upload.
- Batch production: about 10 Shorts a week, made in batches of 5 so the queue never runs dry.
- Weekly: Studio → Earn, note subscribers and hours.
- Every 2 weeks: export the CSV again (or read it from Metricool, connected 13 Nov) and update this plan.

**Chronological order:** Badr → Uhud → the Trench (Ahzab) → Hudaybiyyah ("a clear victory", Al-Fath 48:1) →
Khaybar → Mu'tah → the Conquest of Makkah → Hunayn → Tabuk → Yamamah → Yarmouk → Qadisiyyah → Jerusalem
(Umar (RA)) → Egypt → Nahavand → Sindh (Muhammad bin Qasim) → Spain (Tariq ibn Ziyad) → Hattin and Jerusalem
(Salahuddin) → Ain Jalut → Constantinople (Muhammad al-Fatih). Left out on purpose: conflicts between Muslims
(Jamal, Siffin, Karbala).

**Each film:** background → the battle (maps, strategy, suspense; never graphic) → lessons and recommendations.
**Rules:** the Prophet ﷺ and the Companions are never shown; armies only as distant faceless silhouettes, banners,
dust and maps. Sources on screen: Quran first (Badr: Al-Anfal 8; Uhud: Aal-Imran 3:121–175; the Trench: Al-Ahzab
33:9–27; Hunayn: At-Tawbah 9:25–26; Tabuk: At-Tawbah 9), then sahih hadith, then the standard seerah books, named;
details found only in seerah books are said to be so. No titles that blame a group.

**Anniversaries (Islamic calendar, dates approximate):** films come out in chronological order; on each battle's
anniversary, new Shorts and a community post point back to its film. Badr 17 Ramadan (~24 Feb 2027), Conquest of
Makkah 20 Ramadan (~27 Feb 2027), Uhud mid-Shawwal (~mid-Mar), Hunayn Shawwal (~Mar), the Trench Shawwal/Dhul
Qadah (~Mar–Apr), Khaybar Muharram (~Jun), Mu'tah Jumada al-Ula (~Oct), Tabuk Rajab (~Dec/Jan). Badr and the
Conquest of Makkah must be published before Ramadan 2027.
