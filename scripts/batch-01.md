# Haq Stories — Batch 01 (7 Shorts)

English narration, AI voiceover. Vertical 1080×1920.

**House rules for every Short**
- **Translation:** Sahih International for every on-screen verse. Where it says "Abraham" / "Solomon" / "Joseph", show the name your audience knows in brackets, e.g. "Abraham [Ibrahim]".
- **Arabic:** copy-paste from quran.com. Never retype.
- **Honorifics:** on screen "Hazrat ___ (AS)", Prophet Muhammad "ﷺ". Spoken: "alayhis salaam" / "sallallahu alayhi wa sallam". Check how the AI voice pronounces these in the first render.
- **Titles:** your proven style: a short, surprising story line ending in "…".

## 1. Ibrahim and the fire (sequel to "Why Hazrat Ibrahim (AS) destroyed the idols?")

**Title:** They built a fire to burn him… then Allah gave it one command
**Source:** Surah Al-Anbiya 21:57-70 (fire: 21:68-69)

[ON SCREEN: flames, "They built a fire to burn him…"]
After Hazrat Ibrahim, alayhis salaam, broke their idols, his people were furious.
[ON SCREEN: crowd, shadowed faces, "21:68"]
They said: burn him, and defend your gods.
[ON SCREEN: a huge fire, a small figure thrown in]
They built a fire so large, and threw him into it.
[ON SCREEN: verse 21:69, Arabic + "O fire, be coolness and safety upon Abraham [Ibrahim]."]
Then Allah gave the fire one command: "O fire, be coolness and safety upon Ibrahim."
[ON SCREEN: flames turn to soft light]
The fire obeyed its Creator. It could not harm him.
When Allah is with you, no fire is stronger.
Follow for more stories of the prophets.

About 35 seconds.

## 2. The mother bird (new; replaces Quiz 1)

**Title:** A bird cried for her babies… and the Prophet ﷺ stopped everything
**Source:** Sunan Abi Dawud 2675 (narrated by Abdullah ibn Mas'ud; graded sahih by al-Albani). Confirm on sunnah.com.

[ON SCREEN: a small red bird circling, "A bird cried for her babies…"]
The Companions were on a journey with the Prophet, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam.
[ON SCREEN: a nest with two chicks]
They found a small bird with two chicks, and they took the chicks.
[ON SCREEN: the mother bird flapping her wings above them]
The mother bird came back, flapping her wings, circling over them.
[ON SCREEN: "Who has distressed this bird by taking her young?"]
The Prophet, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam, saw her and said: "Who has distressed this bird by taking her young? Return them to her."
[ON SCREEN: chicks back in the nest]
If he cared for the pain of a little bird, how much more should we care for each other?
Follow for more.

About 35 seconds.

## 3. Quiz: People of the Cave (the one quiz in this batch, as a test)

**Title:** They slept in a cave… how many years? 🤔
**Source:** Surah Al-Kahf 18:25 (years), 18:19 ("a day or part of a day")

[ON SCREEN: cave, "How long did the People of the Cave sleep?"]
Do you know how long the People of the Cave slept?
[ON SCREEN: A 100 years, B 40 years, C 309 years, countdown]
A, one hundred years. B, forty years. C, three hundred and nine years. Three, two, one.
[ON SCREEN: C highlighted, "18:25 — three hundred years and exceeded by nine"]
The answer is C. Surah Al-Kahf says they remained three hundred years, and added nine.
[ON SCREEN: "18:19 — a day or part of a day"]
When they woke up, they thought they had slept only a day, or part of a day.
Comment A, B or C. Did you get it right?

About 30 seconds.

## 4. With hardship comes ease

**Title:** Allah didn't say ease comes AFTER hardship…
**Source:** Surah Ash-Sharh 94:5-6

[ON SCREEN: "Allah didn't say ease comes after hardship…"]
Most of us think ease comes after hardship. But that is not what Allah said.
[ON SCREEN: Arabic of 94:5-6 from quran.com]
[ON SCREEN: "For indeed, with hardship will be ease. Indeed, with hardship will be ease." 94:5-6]
Allah said: "Indeed, with hardship comes ease." And then He said it again.
[ON SCREEN: the word "with" highlighted]
With. Not after.
[ON SCREEN: two lights moving side by side]
Your ease is not waiting at the end of your pain. It is already traveling with it.
Send this to someone who needs it today.

About 30 seconds.

## 5. The crying tree trunk (new; replaces Quiz 2)

**Title:** A tree trunk cried like a child… and the Prophet ﷺ came down to hold it
**Source:** Sahih al-Bukhari 3584 and 3585 (narrated by Jabir ibn Abdullah). Confirm on sunnah.com.

[ON SCREEN: an old palm trunk inside a simple mosque, "A tree trunk cried…"]
In the Prophet's mosque, there was a palm tree trunk.
[ON SCREEN: the Prophet's ﷺ place beside the trunk, no depiction of him]
The Prophet, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam, would lean on it when he gave the sermon.
[ON SCREEN: a new wooden pulpit]
Then the Companions built him a pulpit, and he stood on it instead.
[ON SCREEN: the trunk alone, sound of soft crying]
And the trunk began to cry, like a child, so loudly that everyone in the mosque heard it.
[ON SCREEN: light around the trunk, "Sahih al-Bukhari 3584"]
The Prophet, sallallahu alayhi wa sallam, came down and held it, until it became quiet.
A piece of wood cried for missing him. How much do we miss him?
Follow for more.

About 38 seconds. Never show the Prophet ﷺ: use light, the pulpit and the trunk only.

## 6. Sulaiman's prayer (follow-up to "An ant… spoke to an entire army.")

**Title:** You heard what the ant said… but did you hear what Sulaiman (AS) said back?
**Source:** Surah An-Naml 27:17-19

[ON SCREEN: "You heard what the ant said…"]
You heard what the ant said to its people.
[ON SCREEN: verse 27:18, "O ants, enter your dwellings…"]
"O ants, enter your homes, so Sulaiman and his soldiers do not crush you without realizing."
[ON SCREEN: a vast army of men, jinn and birds, then one tiny ant]
Hazrat Sulaiman, alayhis salaam, the king of an army of men, jinn and birds, heard one tiny ant.
[ON SCREEN: "And he smiled, amused at her speech" 27:19]
He smiled. But he did not boast. He raised his hands and prayed:
[ON SCREEN: 27:19, "My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favor…"]
"My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favor, which You have bestowed upon me and upon my parents."
The most powerful king answered the smallest voice with gratitude.
Follow for more stories of the prophets.

About 40 seconds.

## 7. Yusuf, Part 1

**Title:** His own brothers threw him into a well… (Yusuf, Part 1)
**Source:** Surah Yusuf 12:4-5, 12:8-10, 12:15

[ON SCREEN: "Yusuf (AS) · Part 1"]
His own brothers threw him into a well. This is the story of Hazrat Yusuf, alayhis salaam. Part 1.
[ON SCREEN: eleven stars, the sun and the moon, "12:4"]
As a child, Yusuf saw a dream: eleven stars, the sun and the moon, bowing down to him.
[ON SCREEN: "12:5"]
His father, Hazrat Yaqub, alayhis salaam, told him: do not tell your brothers.
[ON SCREEN: brothers, shadowed faces, "12:8"]
But his brothers already felt their father loved Yusuf more. So they made a plan, and threw him into a well.
[ON SCREEN: dark well, a light appears, "12:15"]
Then Allah told Yusuf: one day, you will tell them about what they did, and they will not know it is you.
Part 2 is tomorrow. Follow so you do not miss it.

About 40 seconds. Post Part 2 the next day.
