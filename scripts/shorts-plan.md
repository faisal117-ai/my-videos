# Haq Stories: Shorts plan (16 Nov 2026)

## What our own data says
- New animated style (Nov): typical Short ~780 views vs ~216 for the old ones; 5 of 12 over 1,000.
- Bright opening + twist on screen beats a dark opening (Yunus 514 in 5½ h, Ayyub 777 in 13 h vs Musa's mother 295
  in 7 h and 30% viewed-vs-swiped).
- Story titles with "…" win; advice titles ("Why…", "Stop…") never got pushed; clip-style and "Part 1" Shorts
  underperform; mercy/hope endings bring the most subscribers; the one Urdu Short did ~1/5 of its English twin.
- In the niche: Shorts are a small share of the big channels' views; the hits are one impossible event or a real
  person ("The man Allah stopped the sun for", 744K) and **recurring named series** (One Islam's "Muslim
  Undertaker", 82 Shorts, 41M views).
- Research (third-party, 2026): "viewed vs swiped" is the gate (aim 70–80%+); Shorts bring many but less loyal
  subscribers; the fix is "bridge" Shorts that stay in the lane of the long films and always carry a Related video.

## The role of Shorts
Discovery and bridge. Every Short is in the lane of one of our four series and points to a film (Related video,
pinned comment). Shorts that do not lead anywhere are not made.

## Five named Shorts series (each with its own look, so the channel never feels templated)
| Series (on-screen label) | What it is | Example | Look |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Moment** | the strongest moment of the coming or latest film, ending on a cliffhanger → the film | "He took the flag when three commanders had fallen…" (Mu'tah, Khalid Part 1) | the film's own scene, full colour |
| **Quran Wonders** | one impossible event from the Quran, weekly | "They slept 309 years…", "An army of elephants stopped at Makkah" | starry night / gold, big Arabic word |
| **Words That Changed History** | one sourced sentence and its story | "Whoever worshipped Muhammad… Muhammad has died; whoever worships Allah, Allah is alive" (Abu Bakr (RA), Bukhari) | parchment, the quote building word by word |
| **Duas of the Prophets** | a prophet's dua from the Quran and the moment he said it (shareable, saved) | Yunus in the fish (21:87), Ayyub (21:83), Musa (28:24), Ibrahim (14:35–41) | calm dawn light, Arabic + translation, ends with "Save this dua" |
| **On This Day** | Islamic-calendar anniversaries | 17 Ramadan: "On this day, 313 faced 1,000…" → Badr film | map + date stamp |

## The craft rules (every Short)
- About 30 s, never over 35; a bright, warm first frame; the main object visible from frame 1.
- Setup and twist both on screen within 2 s; hope or mercy reached early and sealed at the end.
- Title "X… then Y" + one emoji, a number where the source gives one; never an advice title.
- Ends on "Follow for more." so it loops; Arabic only from the packages, sources on screen.
- Never shown: prophets, the Prophet ﷺ, Companions, righteous women, angels.
- **No remakes with near-identical titles** of a Short the channel already has (17 Nov: "Four birds… flew back
  alive" had 4 public views in its first hour next to the older "Four birds… came back alive again"). A topic
  already covered gets a new angle and a clearly different title, or is not made.

- **Opening rule (approved 17 Nov, from the opening test):** the first line names a person and a defiant or
  surprising act, then promises a turn ("…until", "…then Allah"): Pharaoh "He called himself a god… until the sea"
  held 37.8% vs Ayyub 29.8%, four birds 31%, Yunus 19.8%. Never open on a list of suffering or a gruesome detail.
  One hardship line only; the dua or turn by ~6 s (Ayyub lost half its viewers at 7–10 s on a second hardship
  line). The first frame must really be lit: check every render's cover frame (Yunus went out near-black).

## Adopted from Miracle of Islam (17 Nov, user: "copy all good things"; see competitor-analysis.md part 4)
We copy their topics, formats and habits, never their videos, scripts, titles or clips (reused content breaks
YouTube's rules), and only topics with a sound source.
1. **Their proven topics, our own scripts.** Sourced ones go into the Shorts queue (one or two a week):
   - the three men trapped in the cave by a rock, each praying by one good deed (Bukhari 2272, Muslim 2743);
   - the leper, the bald man and the blind man, tested with wealth (Bukhari 3464, Muslim 2964);
   - the man who sent 1,000 dinars across the sea in a piece of wood (Bukhari 2291);
   - Uwais al-Qarani, honoured for serving his mother (Muslim 2542; a Tabi'i: "rahimahullah");
   - Abu Lubaba (RA) tying himself to a pillar of the mosque (seerah and tafsir of 9:102, said so);
   - Dawud (AS) and the two men who climbed into his prayer room (Sad 38:21–25);
   - Ashab al-Kahf, Khidr and the boat, the cow (already planned).
   Skipped (no reliable source): Musa's neighbour in Paradise, one bread vs 70 years of worship, Salahuddin and
   the Christian girl, and their invented "news" stories.
2. **Title test, 50-50 (user, 17 Nov):** 7 of 14 Shorts a week get their style, a short question about a moment
   in the story with one emoji ("Why Did He Tie Himself to a Pillar? 😢"); 7 keep our "X… then Y". One of each
   every day, swapping slots daily. Story questions only, never advice questions ("Why Is Charity Powerful?":
   our advice titles went 0 for 14). Same opening rules and length for both. Decide after 2 weeks (~14 each) on
   views and "stayed to watch"; the winner gets 70% or more. Never a title near-identical to theirs or ours.
3. **Companions and righteous people in Shorts,** not only prophets (Umar, Ali, Abu Lubaba, Uwais): their recent
   Shorts are mostly these, and it feeds our Companions series.
4. **Evergreen question films** (their two biggest long videos, 245K and 171K, are everyday questions): one
   10–15 min Quran-wonders film now and then, built on a question people search, e.g. "Why did Allah mention
   honey in the Quran?" (An-Nahl 16:68–69), "What happened to the army of the Elephant?" (Al-Fil).
5. **Volume and consistency, never a gap:** their 60 daily Shorts in Mar–Apr 2026 built the channel; after a
   2-month gap their Shorts fell ~6× and never recovered. We keep at least 7 days of Shorts scheduled ahead in
   Metricool at all times; if the queue drops below that, making Shorts comes before anything else.
6. **Captions test:** big word-by-word captions, centred, one highlighted word (their style), on half of a
   week's Shorts vs our current text cards; compare "stayed to watch" at the Friday review.
7. **Compilations of our own work** (from mid-December, once there are enough films and Shorts): e.g.
   "Stories of the Prophets: 1 Hour" built from our films and re-voiced links, with a new intro, chapters and
   end screen, so it is a new, useful video and not a re-upload. They earn long-video watch hours.
8. **Film thumbnails (user's rule, 18 Nov): always the style of YouTube's suggested Urdu Yusuf thumbnail**
   (`thumbs/yt-yusuf-ur-well-throne.png`): a rich, detailed illustrated scene in warm light; a split or contrast
   that shows the story's turn (dark hardship → bright help from Allah); a big two-line headline (the hardship…
   / …the turn) in the film's language, the turn in yellow; a small chip with the prophet's name and honorific
   (حضرت یوسفؑ / Prophet Yusuf (AS)); the key object glowing. No people ever; honest words, no news badges, no
   real faces, no shock text. Made with YouTube Studio's thumbnail generator from a prompt and headline Claude
   writes for each film (Claude checks the result), or drawn by Claude when the generator can't.
Not copied: sensational, unverifiable stories; hype ("SHOCKED everyone", "TERRIFIED the world").

Ramadan: "30 Nights, 30 Stories" replaces the 18:30 Shorts on weekdays.

## Urdu/Hindi Shorts: a careful test
Two a week ("The Moment" from the Urdu film, Thu and Sat), pointing to the Urdu film. Keep only if they reach
half of the English Shorts' views within 4 weeks.

## What we measure every Friday
- Viewed vs swiped (goal 70%+), average % viewed (goal 90%+, loops can pass 100%).
- Subscribers per 1,000 views, and clicks from the Related video to the film.
- A named series that stays below the channel's typical Short after 6 tries is replaced.

## A channel to be remembered
- **Trust above all:** every verse and hadith sourced on screen; no invented details; never a misleading title.
- **One signature:** the same gold-and-night palette, the حق کہانیاں mark, the same narrator, the same last line
  ("…and Allah's promise is true" at the end of films), a short sound logo.
- **A library, not a feed:** complete series in order (prophets, battles, Companions, heroes, later the Seerah),
  timeless stories that are still watched in 10 years.
- **Never miss a day**, reply to people, ask them which story comes next.
- **Mercy first:** every story ends in hope; nothing that divides Muslims.
