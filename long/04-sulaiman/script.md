## 0. Hook (0:00–0:35)
1. A queen's throne crossed hundreds of miles… before an eye could blink.
2. And the king who received it did not boast. He said: "This is from the favour of my Lord, to test me."
3. This is the story of Prophet Sulaiman, alayhis salaam: the king who understood the birds, heard an ant, commanded the wind…
4. …and how one grateful heart turned a whole nation from the sun to Allah.
5. But first, a question: what does a man say, when Allah gives him everything?
6. *(Title card)* The King Who Was Grateful: The Story of Prophet Sulaiman (AS).

## 1. The Son of Dawud (how did a young man out-judge a king?)
1. Who was Sulaiman, and why did Allah choose him?
2. He was the son of Prophet Dawud, alayhis salaam, the king of Bani Israil. Allah says: "And to David We gave Solomon. An excellent servant, indeed he was one repeatedly turning back to Allah." *(38:30)*
3. While he was still young, two men came to his father's court. One man's sheep had gone into another man's field at night and eaten the crop.
4. Dawud, alayhis salaam, judged. Then the young Sulaiman offered a judgement too.
5. And Allah says: "We gave understanding of the case to Solomon, and to each of them We gave judgement and knowledge." *(21:79)*
6. Understanding from Allah: that was the first gift. It would not be the last.

## 2. A Kingdom Like No Other (what did he ask Allah for?)
1. When his father passed away, Sulaiman inherited his prophethood and his kingdom. *(27:16)*
2. And he made a dua that no king had made before: "My Lord, forgive me, and grant me a kingdom such as will not belong to anyone after me. Indeed, You are the Bestower." *(38:35)*
3. He asked for forgiveness first. Then for the kingdom.
4. And Allah answered. "So We subjected to him the wind, blowing by his command, gently, wherever he directed." *(38:36)*
5. A morning's journey on that wind was a month's travel. An evening's journey was another month. *(34:12)*
6. And Allah made a spring of molten copper flow for him, and the jinn worked for him by his Lord's permission: high palaces, great bowls like reservoirs, cooking pots fixed to the ground. *(34:12–13)*
7. But with all of it came one command: "Work, O family of David, in gratitude. And few of My servants are grateful." *(34:13)*
8. Few are grateful. Would Sulaiman be one of the few?

## 3. The Language of the Birds (what did he say about his gifts?)
1. Sulaiman stood before his people and said: "O people, we have been taught the language of birds, and we have been given from all things. Indeed, this is evident bounty." *(27:16)*
2. Not "I earned it." Not "I am great." Evident bounty: a gift, from Allah.
3. Remember those words. Because later in this story, a man who was given wealth will say the opposite… and we will see where each one ended.
4. And so his army gathered: "And gathered for Solomon were his soldiers of the jinn, and men, and birds, and they were marching in rows." *(27:17)*
5. One day, that army marched toward a valley… and the smallest creature in it spoke.

## 4. The Valley of the Ants (what can one ant teach a king?)
1. What would you do, if an army that size was coming toward your home?
2. "Until, when they came upon the valley of the ants, an ant said: O ants, enter your dwellings, so that Solomon and his soldiers do not crush you while they do not perceive." *(27:18)*
3. One small ant. She warned her people. And she even made an excuse for the army: "while they do not perceive." They would not mean to harm you.
4. And Sulaiman, alayhis salaam, heard her.
5. "So he smiled, amused at her speech." *(27:19)*
6. A king with the wind and the jinn at his command… stopped for an ant.
7. And then he made a dua. Save this one: "My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favour which You have bestowed upon me and upon my parents, and to do righteousness of which You approve. And admit me, by Your mercy, among Your righteous servants." *(27:19)*
8. He did not ask for more power. He asked to be grateful.
9. But one bird in his army… was missing.

## 5. The Missing Hoopoe (where had the bird gone?)
1. Sulaiman, alayhis salaam, inspected the birds. "Why do I not see the hoopoe, or is he among the absent?" *(27:20)*
2. A king who notices one small bird missing. He said the hoopoe would be punished, unless it brought a clear reason. *(27:21)*
3. *(pause)* Then the hoopoe came back.
4. "I have learned what you have not learned, and I have come to you from Sheba with certain news." *(27:22)*
5. What news could a bird bring to a prophet?

## 6. The Queen of Sheba (what had the hoopoe seen?)
1. "Indeed, I found a woman ruling them, and she has been given of all things, and she has a great throne." *(27:23)*
2. A rich land in the south. A great throne. A strong army.
3. "I found her and her people prostrating to the sun instead of Allah." *(27:24)*
4. A whole nation, every morning, bowing to the sun. The sun that Allah Himself had made.
5. Sulaiman did not send an army. He said: "We will see whether you were truthful, or were of the liars." *(27:27)*
6. And he wrote a letter.

## 7. The Letter (what did one letter do to a kingdom?)
1. "Take this letter of mine and deliver it to them. Then leave them, and see what answer they return." *(27:28)*
2. The hoopoe flew back to Sheba and dropped the letter before the queen.
3. She called her chiefs: "O chiefs, indeed a noble letter has been delivered to me. It is from Solomon, and it reads: In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful. Do not be haughty with me, but come to me in submission." *(27:29–31)*
4. Her chiefs said: we are strong, we are fighters, but the decision is yours. *(27:33)*
5. And she was wise. She said: "Indeed, kings, when they enter a city, they ruin it." *(27:34)* She did not want war.
6. "I will send them a gift, and see with what the messengers return." *(27:35)*
7. A gift of wealth… to a man Allah had given everything. How would he answer?

## 8. The Gift (can gold buy a prophet?)
1. The messengers arrived with the queen's treasure.
2. And Sulaiman, alayhis salaam, said: "Do you provide me with wealth? But what Allah has given me is better than what He has given you. Rather, it is you who rejoice in your gift." *(27:36)*
3. He sent the gift back.
4. And the queen understood: this was not an ordinary king. She set out herself, to see him.
5. Then Sulaiman asked his court something that sounds impossible.

## 9. Before an Eye Could Blink (who could bring a throne in a moment?)
1. "O assembly, which of you will bring me her throne before they come to me in submission?" *(27:38)*
2. A powerful one of the jinn said: "I will bring it to you before you rise from your place, and indeed I am strong and trustworthy for it." *(27:39)*
3. Before you stand up. That was the jinn's offer.
4. *(pause)* But a man who had knowledge from the Book said: "I will bring it to you before your glance returns to you." *(27:40)*
5. Before your eye can blink.
6. And there it was. The queen's great throne, from Sheba, standing in front of him.
7. Now remember the question from the start: what does a man say when Allah gives him everything?
8. Sulaiman said: "This is from the favour of my Lord, to test me whether I will be grateful or ungrateful. And whoever is grateful, his gratitude is only for himself. And whoever is ungrateful, then indeed my Lord is Free of need and Generous." *(27:40)*
9. A throne from a thousand miles away… and his first thought was: this is a test.

## 10. The Floor of Glass (what did the queen finally see?)
1. When the queen arrived, she was shown a throne. "Is your throne like this?" She said: "It is as though it were it." *(27:42)*
2. Then she was told to enter the palace. She saw what looked like deep water, and gathered her robe to cross. *(27:44)*
3. But it was a floor of smooth glass, with the water flowing beneath it.
4. Her throne carried in a blink. A palace of glass over water. And a king who gave everything to Allah.
5. And the queen, who had bowed to the sun all her life, said:
6. "My Lord, indeed I have wronged myself, and I submit with Solomon to Allah, Lord of the worlds." *(27:44)*
7. Not to Sulaiman. With Sulaiman, to Allah.
8. A letter, a gift sent back, and a grateful heart: that is how a whole nation turned from the sun to its Creator.

## 11. The Staff (how did the jinn not know?)
1. Sulaiman, alayhis salaam, ruled for many years. And then came the one thing no kingdom can stop.
2. "And when We decreed for him death, nothing showed the jinn his death except a creature of the earth, eating his staff." *(34:14)*
3. He was leaning on his staff when he passed away, and the jinn kept working, thinking he was watching them.
4. Until a small creature ate through the staff, and he fell.
5. "Then it became clear to the jinn that if they had known the unseen, they would not have remained in humiliating work." *(34:14)*
6. The jinn, who could carry a throne before a man could stand up… did not know the unseen. Only Allah knows it.

## 12. The Lesson
1. Allah gave Sulaiman a kingdom no one will ever have again. And the word he kept saying was: thanks.
2. Remember Qarun? He was given treasure and said: "I was only given it because of knowledge I have." The earth swallowed him. *(28:78, 28:81)*
3. Sulaiman was given far more, and said: "This is from the favour of my Lord." *(27:40)*
4. Same test. Two answers.
5. So today, look at one thing Allah gave you: your health, your family, your home, and say Sulaiman's dua: "My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favour." *(27:19)*
